About Me

I am an experienced Data Front-End Developer with over 7 years of professional experience delivering high-quality, scalable, and data-driven web applications. I specialize in building clean, maintainable, and high-performance user interfaces for analytics platforms, dashboards, and enterprise data products.

My expertise includes modern front-end frameworks such as React and Angular, TypeScript, and industry best practices for front-end architecture, performance optimization, and accessibility. I have a strong background in collaborating with UX designers, data engineers, and backend teams to translate complex data and business requirements into intuitive, user-friendly interfaces.

I have hands-on experience integrating front-end applications with RESTful APIs, handling large data sets, and creating interactive data visualizations using libraries such as D3.js and Chart.js. Throughout my career, I have contributed to projects across multiple industries, including technology, e-commerce, healthcare, education, and enterprise systems, delivering solutions that emphasize usability, scalability, and maintainability.

Known for my strong problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and quality-focused mindset, I am committed to continuous learning and staying current with modern front-end technologies to deliver reliable, future-ready data experiences for organizations.
